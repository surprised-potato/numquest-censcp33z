Problem 1 · Linear regression10 pts

Concrete mix design

A ready-mix plant is preparing a trial-mix report for a 4-storey school. The structural engineer specifies a 28-day strength of 40 MPa. Six trial mixes were tested:

Water–cement ratio, \(x\)0.350.400.500.550.600.70
Strength, \(y\) (MPa)48.244.136.533.029.422.8
Required  Model: \(y = a_0 + a_1 x\)
  1. Using the normal equations, find \(a_0\) and \(a_1\). (4)
  2. Compute \(S_t\), \(S_r\) and \(r^2\). (3)
  3. Predict the strength at \(x = 0.45\), and find the largest water–cement ratio that still meets 40 MPa. (3)
Problem 2 · Numerical differentiation10 pts

Detention pond during a storm

A subdivision drains into a pond with vertical walls, plan area \(A = 2{,}500\ \text{m}^2\). The operator read the staff gauge every 10 min:

Time, \(t\) (min)0102030405060
Depth, \(h\) (m)1.201.321.511.762.052.302.47

At \(t = 30\) min the outlet released \(Q_\text{out} = 0.40\ \text{m}^3/\text{s}\).  Storm inflow: \(\;Q_\text{in} = A\,\dfrac{dh}{dt} + Q_\text{out}\)

Required
  1. Estimate \(dh/dt\) at \(t = 30\) min by 2-point forward, backward and central differences (m/min). (5)
  2. Using the central value, compute \(Q_\text{in}\) at \(t = 30\) min in m³/s. (3)
  3. Which estimate would you report, and why? State its truncation-error order. (2)
Problem 3 · Numerical integration10 pts

Stream gauging for an irrigation intake

From the left bank, a hydrographer records depth \(d\) and depth-averaged velocity \(v\) every 2 m across a 20 m creek:

Station, \(s\) (m)02468101214161820
Depth, \(d\) (m)00.61.11.51.81.91.71.41.00.50
Velocity, \(v\) (m/s)00.320.550.710.820.860.800.680.500.300

\(A = \displaystyle\int_0^{20} d\,ds \qquad Q = \int_0^{20} v\,d\,ds\)

Required
  1. By the composite trapezoidal rule, compute the flow area \(A\) and discharge \(Q\). (5)
  2. Compute the mean velocity \(\bar v = Q/A\). (1)
  3. By the cumulative trapezoidal rule, tabulate the discharge from the left bank to each station; interpolate the station that splits the flow in half. (4)
