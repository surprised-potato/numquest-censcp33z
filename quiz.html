1. Concrete mix design: linear regression10 pts

A ready-mix plant is preparing a trial-mix report for a 4-storey school building. The structural engineer specifies a 28-day compressive strength of 40 MPa. The plant tested six trial mixes with different water–cement ratios and recorded the average 28-day cylinder strength of each:

Water–cement ratio, \(x\)0.350.400.500.550.600.70
Strength, \(y\) (MPa)48.244.136.533.029.422.8
Table 1. Trial-mix results (average of 3 cylinders each)
Given

The six data points above; a straight-line model \(y = a_0 + a_1 x\).

Required
  1. Using the normal equations, find \(a_0\) and \(a_1\). (4 pts)
  2. Compute \(S_t\), \(S_r\) and the coefficient of determination \(r^2\). (3 pts)
  3. Predict the strength of a mix with \(x = 0.45\), and find the largest water–cement ratio that still meets the 40 MPa specification. (3 pts)

2. Detention pond during a storm: numerical differentiation10 pts

A subdivision drains into a detention pond with nearly vertical walls, so its plan area stays at \(A = 2{,}500\ \text{m}^2\) at every depth. During a storm, the operator read the staff gauge every 10 minutes:

Time, \(t\) (min)0102030405060
Depth, \(h\) (m)1.201.321.511.762.052.302.47
Table 2. Staff-gauge readings, \(\Delta t = 10\) min

At \(t = 30\) min the outlet pipe was releasing \(Q_\text{out} = 0.40\ \text{m}^3/\text{s}\). By conservation of volume, the storm inflow is

$$Q_\text{in} = A\,\frac{dh}{dt} + Q_\text{out}.$$
Required
  1. Estimate \(dh/dt\) at \(t = 30\) min with the 2-point forward, backward and central difference formulas, in m/min. (5 pts)
  2. Using the central-difference value, compute the storm inflow \(Q_\text{in}\) at \(t = 30\) min in m³/s. (3 pts)
  3. Which of the three estimates would you report, and why? State the order of its truncation error. (2 pts)

Station, \(s\) (m)02468101214161820
Depth, \(d\) (m)00.61.11.51.81.91.71.41.00.50
Velocity, \(v\) (m/s)00.320.550.710.820.860.800.680.500.300
Table 3. Cross-section survey, \(\Delta s = 2\) m (left bank at \(s = 0\))

The flow area and discharge are

$$A = \int_0^{20} d\,ds, \qquad Q = \int_0^{20} v\,d\,ds.$$
Required
  1. Using the composite trapezoidal rule, compute the flow area \(A\) and the discharge \(Q\). (5 pts)
  2. Compute the mean velocity \(\bar v = Q/A\). (1 pt)
